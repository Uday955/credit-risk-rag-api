Credit History and FICO Score Policy

An applicant's FICO score and length of credit history are key indicators of 
creditworthiness. FICO scores below 670 are generally classified as "fair" or 
"poor" credit, and are associated with a higher historical likelihood of default. 
Scores above 740 are considered "very good" to "exceptional" and are associated 
with lower risk.

Credit history length also matters: applicants with a longer history of credit 
use (typically 10+ years) have a more extensive track record for assessing 
repayment reliability, compared to applicants with limited credit history. 
Recent credit-seeking behavior, such as multiple new accounts opened in a short 
period, can also indicate increased financial stress and is factored into risk 
assessment.

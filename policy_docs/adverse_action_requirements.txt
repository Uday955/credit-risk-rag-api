Adverse Action Notice Requirements (Based on the Equal Credit Opportunity Act - ECOA)

Under the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) and Regulation B, creditors are 
required to notify applicants when an application for credit is denied, and to 
provide specific, accurate reasons for the denial - a general statement such as 
"you do not meet our underwriting criteria" is not sufficient.

Adverse action notices must identify the specific principal reasons for the 
denial, drawn from the actual factors that adversely affected the credit decision. 
Common disclosed reasons include: insufficient income, excessive existing 
obligations relative to income, limited credit history, delinquent credit 
obligations, and length of employment. Explanations must be specific enough that 
an applicant can understand the basis for the decision and take corrective action 
if desired, rather than receiving a vague or generic denial.

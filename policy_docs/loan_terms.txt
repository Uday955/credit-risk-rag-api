Loan Term and Amount Policy

Loan term length (e.g., 36 months vs. 60 months) directly affects risk assessment. 
Longer loan terms carry greater uncertainty over the repayment period, including 
exposure to potential changes in the applicant's income, employment, or financial 
circumstances over a longer timeframe. As a result, longer-term loans are 
generally associated with higher risk profiles compared to shorter-term loans of 
similar size.

Loan amount relative to income and existing obligations is also assessed. Loans 
that represent a larger financial commitment relative to the applicant's income 
and existing debt are evaluated more conservatively.
